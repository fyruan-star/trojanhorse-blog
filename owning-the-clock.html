Owning theClock

HOW A LONGEVITY TEST CHANGED HANDS
FOR AN UNDISCLOSED SUM

Francis Ruan

Infinite Epigenetics announced the acquisition of Tally Health on 29 April 2026 as an asset purchase. Terms were not disclosed by either party. Tally Health was co-founded in 2023 by the Harvard geneticist David Sinclair and sells an epigenetic biological age test alongside a line of supplements. The buyer also owns TruDiagnostic and states that it holds among the largest private datasets of adult DNA methylation. Tally Health continues to operate as a standalone consumer brand under Melanie Goldey, and Matthew Dawson remains chief executive of Infinite Epigenetics.

No consideration was disclosed by either party.
