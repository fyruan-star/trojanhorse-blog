AMD andXilinx

WHAT AN ALL-STOCK TRANSACTION ACTUALLY IS,
AND WHY ITS HEADLINE VALUE MOVES

Francis Ruan

AMD agreed to acquire Xilinx in October 2020 in an all-stock transaction valued at approximately $35 billion at announcement. It completed in February 2022 at a figure widely reported near $49 billion. Xilinx's business had not changed to account for the difference.

What all-stock means

No cash left AMD. Xilinx shareholders gave up their shares and received newly issued AMD shares at a fixed ratio of 1.7234 AMD shares for each Xilinx share.

The important consequence is that the seller does not exit. Xilinx shareholders became owners of the combined company and continue to bear its results, good or bad. That is a different arrangement from a cash sale and it changes how both sides think about price, because a seller taking stock is accepting a view on the buyer as well as on its own business.

AMD's share price had risen very substantially over the preceding years. That is what made a purchase of this size available to a company which had been in serious difficulty not long before.

Why the number moved

The exchange ratio was fixed in October 2020. AMD's share price was not. Between announcement and completion in February 2022 AMD stock rose, and the value of the consideration rose with it.

So the $35 billion reported at announcement was AMD's share price on one day multiplied by the number of shares to be issued. Treating it as a price paid will mislead you, and published comparisons of what companies in a sector are worth frequently make exactly this error.

The business acquired

Xilinx made field programmable gate arrays, chips whose logic can be reconfigured after manufacture. They are slower and more expensive per unit of work than a chip designed for one fixed purpose, and they suit applications where the task changes or the volume does not justify a custom design. Telecommunications equipment was the largest market.

AMD's stated case concerned a broader data centre product range. The business has since been reported within AMD's embedded segment, and I would treat claims about its contribution to AMD's AI revenue carefully, because the company does not break it out in a way that settles the question.

In an all-stock deal the announced value is the buyer's share price on a single day, and the seller has taken an ownership position rather than been paid.

Sources

  2. Completed 14 February 2022. The higher figure at completion reflects AMD share price appreciation and is widely reported at around $49bn.
  3. AMD does not separately disclose Xilinx-derived revenue in a form permitting precise attribution, and this note does not attempt one.
